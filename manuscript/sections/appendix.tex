\section{Reproduction}
\label{app:repro}
Every result in this paper can be regenerated from raw logs. Table~\ref{tab:repro} lists what bit-for-bit reproduction needs. We checked it three ways. Before the NumPy pin, runners with and without AVX-512 disagreed on every run of one experiment (0 of 50 matched). After the pin, a probe experiment gave identical hashes on an Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C and on AMD EPYC 9V74, 9V45 and 7763 (the last without AVX-512), under Ubuntu 22.04 and 24.04. And the full sweep, re-executed on independently provisioned runners, matched 2{,}366 of 2{,}366 runs, with identical tables.

\begin{table}[h]
\caption{Requirements to reproduce the results bit for bit.}
\label{tab:repro}
\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}P{2.8cm}P{9.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
CPU & x86-64 with fused multiply-add (every Intel and AMD CPU since 2013). AVX-512 is neither required nor used: the pin selects the SSE4 baseline, so AVX2 and AVX-512 machines agree. \\
Operating system & Linux with the GNU C library (tested: Ubuntu 22.04 and 24.04, glibc 2.35 and 2.39). \\
Software & Python 3.11 and the dependency versions in the committed lockfile (NumPy 2.x, SciPy with HiGHS, pandas, PyArrow); \texttt{NPY\_DISABLE\_CPU\_FEATURES="X86\_V4 X86\_V3 AVX512\_ICL AVX512\_SPR"}, set by the package, the Makefile and the CI workflows. \\
Special hardware & None: no TPM, reboot or BIOS access. The calibration bundle is committed. \\
Compute & 21 experiments, 2{,}366 runs. About four hours as 21 parallel jobs on hosted runners; compressed raw logs are 0.1--0.7\,GB per experiment. \\
Not covered & ARM, other operating systems and other C libraries are not bit-comparable; for them the portable claim is statistical (ten paired seeds). Wall-clock columns vary by machine and are excluded from the hashes. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\noindent With the lockfile installed, the following regenerates the runs, compares them with the recorded hashes and rebuilds this document.
{\small
\begin{verbatim}
uv sync --frozen
make paper          # 21 specs x 10 seeds
make paper-verify   # compare hashes
voss repro runs/E09/<run>/manifest.json
make manuscript
\end{verbatim}}
